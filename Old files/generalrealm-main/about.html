About EighthBrain

Backing bold innovators, shaping tomorrow's industries, creating lasting impact.

At EighthBrain, we partner with the boldest innovators and trailblazers, helping startups unlock their full potential and achieve extraordinary results. Let’s shape the future, together.

Strategic Partnership Beyond Capital

We believe successful venture investing requires more than just funding. Our approach is built on three pillars.

Strategic Funding

We invest from pre-seed to Series A, aligning capital with long-term value. Our structures are flexible, founder-friendly, and focused on sustainable growth.

Expert Mentorship

We work hands-on with founders—offering guidance across strategy, product, go-to-market, hiring, and fundraising. We bring real experience, not just advice.

Global Network Access

Our portfolio companies gain access to a trusted global network of investors, customers, and strategic partners—accelerating momentum and unlocking scale.
