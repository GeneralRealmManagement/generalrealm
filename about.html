our story

WE'VE BEEN ON
BOTH SIDES
of the table

GeneralRealm was founded in 2024 by operators who built and ran companies before they invested. We raised rounds, shipped products, made payroll, made mistakes — and learned the gap between what founders actually need and what most VCs provide.

That gap is why we exist. We're set up to be the partner we wish we'd had.

built by operators, for operators
Operator-led Built before we backed. Every partner shipped products, ran sales calls, and made payroll before they wrote a check.

Pre-seed first We lead the first round. Conviction capital, written when the spreadsheet is still TBD — not follow-on tickets dressed up as a thesis.
